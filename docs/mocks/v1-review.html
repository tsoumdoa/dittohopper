Viewport HUD is a Rhino DisplayConduit drawing in 2D over the view. It's display-only: you can't click it, so every action lives in the panel. Its tags follow the objects as you orbit, and a change too small to see (the 0.04 mm nudge) gets a ring.
Review panel is a dockable Eto panel. It opens from a Rhino command (DittoReview: this doc vs its last save, or vs a file) and from GH via Open Review. You don't need GH to review. Hovering a row highlights its objects; selecting one dims the rest.
Review state (ticks, “intended”, sign-off) is saved with the result, so a team can sign off rev B → current. The idea chip is a proposal: the engine doesn't detect splits today.
